Privacy

This page explains what data is processed when you visit eikeduevel.com. The site sets no cookies. Apart from the visitor counter described below, it loads nothing from other websites.

Who is responsible

Eike Düvel, eike.duevel@gmail.com. The postal address is in the legal notice.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. This data may be processed in the United States. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in running the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details are in the GitHub privacy statement.

Visitor statistics

I use GoatCounter to count visits. GoatCounter stores nothing on your device and keeps no record of individual visitors. It does not store your IP address. It uses the IP address and browser information in memory only, for at most eight hours, to tell repeat visits apart and to derive your country. What is stored are aggregate counts: which pages were viewed, which sites visitors came from, and totals of browsers, operating systems and countries.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, the counter is not loaded and your visit is not counted.

GoatCounter is run by Martin Tournoij (Ireland). Its data is stored on servers in Finland and Germany. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing how many people visit the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GoatCounter privacy policy.

Fonts

The fonts are served from this site. Your browser does not contact any font service.

Links to other sites

Links to publishers, journals and event pages lead to other websites. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Your rights

You can ask me for access to, correction or deletion of data about you, ask me to restrict its processing, and object to it. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg.
